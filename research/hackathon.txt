## **Track 2 · Stable Agents: Open Corridors · $2,000 in CELO**

Agents that move value between currencies on Celo mainnet: quoting and settling FX on Textile across cNGN, wARS, wBRL and USDC against USD₮, or charging and paying in USA₮, Tether’s US-regulated dollar, through Celo’s x402 facilitator. Treasury agents, smarter FX pricing, pay-per-call APIs, cross-border payments. Two subtracks, each with its own prizes:

**Textile FX**  
🥇**1st Prize: $750**  
🥈**2nd Prize: $250**

[Textile](https://app.textilecredit.com/?utm_campaign=agents-on-open-rails-hackathon-starts-tuesday-october-6th&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=celo-devs.beehiiv.com) is a permissionless onchain FX marketplace. Most quotes there today follow a fixed spread, which leaves room for agents that price smarter: inventory-aware pricing, FX risk management, capital-efficient quoting, or a treasury agent that quotes to manage a business’s own currency exposure rather than to earn the spread. Live on Celo: cNGN, wARS, wBRL, and USDC against USD₮.

**USA₮ with x402**  
🥇**1st Prize: $750**  
🥈**2nd Prize: $250**

[USA₮](https://usat.io/?utm_campaign=agents-on-open-rails-hackathon-starts-tuesday-october-6th&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=celo-devs.beehiiv.com) is Tether’s US-regulated dollar stablecoin, and Celo is the first chain after Ethereum to carry it. Builders can claim it from the Google Cloud Web3 faucet by proving they’re human with Self, without handing over their data. That makes Celo the only chain where a regulated dollar, a privacy-preserving identity layer, and a machine payment rail are all live together.

## **Track 3 · Build with buy · $1,000 in CELO across 5 projects (5 x $200)**

[buy](https://usebuy.ai/?utm_campaign=agents-on-open-rails-hackathon-starts-tuesday-october-6th&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=celo-devs.beehiiv.com) turns HTTP 402 Payment Required into a working status code: a stablecoin payment signed locally on Celo, attached to the retry, and optionally a proof that a real human is behind it. In practice, your agent pays for a service the moment it needs it, in stablecoins, settled on Celo in seconds, with no accounts, API keys, or subscriptions. You can use it from the command line, plug it into Claude.

Services available today include:

*   Disposable Linux machines by the hour

*   Isolated agent browsers by the minute

*   Social data from Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Reddit, and X

*   Flight search and flight status

*   RPCs, indexer, inference

Build a product whose core flow pays for services your team doesn’t own, and solve a real problem. Examples: a creator-analytics agent that pulls TikTok and YouTube data on demand, a recruiting assistant that checks candidates on LinkedIn, or an agent that rents a browser to finish a task for its user. Judged on the problem you solve and how deep the integration goes. Spamming the cheapest call scores nothing.
